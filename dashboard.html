📦 Add inventory

Type a product name. If it already exists, this restocks it — adds to whatever count it already has. If it's brand new, it creates the product for you. No spreadsheet needed.
Got a whole restock list at once?
Paste your ENTIRE list in one go — you don't need to split it up by category. Keep your own section headers in there (like "Lips (Lip Contour Stains)" or just "Prestige" / "Essentials" as a divider) and the system reads them as you go — everything under a header gets tagged with it, until the next header changes it. A header it doesn't recognize (like a generic "BEAUTY PRODUCT" label) is just ignored, nothing breaks. Any of these line styles work: Lip Gloss Pink, 12, a line copied from a spreadsheet, or your own style like Lip Gloss Pink -12. Know the price already? Add it with AED/DHS instead of a plain number, like Huda Beauty Lip Stain - Pink Blush -180 AED — quantity defaults to 1 for that style (use the comma format with a 5th field, e.g. Lip Gloss Pink, 3, Lips, Prestige, 95.50, if you want both a quantity and a price on one line). If you'd rather do one category/tier at a time instead, the Tier and Category pickers below cover anything that never hits a header.
